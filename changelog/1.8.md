# 1.8 — 2026-11-19

## Added

- **Statuses.** Give any conversation a status: **Needs reply**, **Waiting on others**, **Can archive** or **Long-term value**. Set it from the pill above a message, the status button in the title bar, or the right-click menu. Each status has one colour, used everywhere.
- **Today.** The app opens on a home screen with a snapshot of the day: how many conversations are in each status, what needs your reply (with what is due today), and who you are waiting on, with Reply, Follow up and Nudge buttons.
- **Status board.** A column for each status. Drag a conversation to another column to change its status, open it with a click, or archive everything in **Can archive** at once. Filter the board by account.
- **Sort unsorted mail.** Suggests a status for inbox mail that has none. With an assistant connected the model reads each message; without one, simple rules sort it. A setting can archive **Can archive** mail as soon as it is sorted.
- **Sent replies move on.** Sending a reply to a conversation marked **Needs reply** moves it to **Waiting on others**.
- **Inbox filter.** All, Needs reply, Waiting and Unsorted pills above the inbox.
- **Assistant settings.** **Settings → Assistant** connects Claude (Anthropic) or Codex (OpenAI), and chooses what the assistant does with statuses: suggest them, draft replies, show a card on Today.
- **Folder colours and a Folders tab.** Pick a colour for each folder in **Settings → Accounts → Folders**; it shows in the sidebar. Rename, delete and add folders for every account in one place.
- **Accounts that need attention.** An account that has to sign in again says so in Settings, with a **Sign in again** button, and shows its unread count.
- **Go menu.** ⌘1 Today, ⌘2 Status Board, ⌘3 Inbox.

## Changed

- The old **Today** list of unread mail from today is now **Unread Today**.
- A lighter, bluer look: selected rows, pills and the accent colour follow the new status palette.
